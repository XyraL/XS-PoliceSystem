# Changelog

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-09

The first release.

- Stations, prisons and impound lots built in game, for any number of departments
- A builder with an overview of what is missing, a to-scale plan of every place, one-click Mission Row PD, Paleto Bay Sheriff and Bolingbroke, and Ctrl+K search
- About 150 settings changed in game, live for everyone with no restart
- Places can be switched off without deleting them
- A boss office: roster, ranks, callsigns, hours, notes, warnings and commendations, the station's armory and garage, the account and an activity log
- Works with the evidences script, or turn our evidence off for another one
- Field notes on evidence: how fresh it is, the gun and any print
- A lab bench: line up casings under a microscope, run DNA, overlay prints. Reports link to earlier scenes
- Prints & DNA shows warrants, bookings and lab links
- Prison guards who patrol and search inmates, a commissary and an inmate trader
- Commissary credit from prison jobs, spent on food, cigarettes and phone calls
- Scrap from prison jobs and a workbench for shivs, lockpicks and wire cutters. A shiv fights like a knife
- Tunnels dug with a spoon and fences cut with wire cutters
- A stash under every cell mattress that stays between sentences, and hidden stash spots placed in the builder
- Nothing picked up in prison leaves with them. Their own items come back at release
- Duty, lockers, an armory by rank with item photos, garage and helipad, numbered evidence lockers, booking and a boss office
- Impound to a lot with a hold time, a fee and an optional police hold. Owners pay and collect at the lot's desk
- Map blips designed in the builder, shown to everyone, police, or police on duty
- Floor markers at points can be switched on or off for each place
- Prison jobs made in the builder: what each one looks like, how long it takes, the time off and its spots. Five come ready, and the Bolingbroke layout fills in a whole prison
- Workout stations in prison, and canteen meals set per prison
- An MDT button on the wheel that opens XS-MDT, ps-mdt, redutzu-mdt, kartik-mdt, tk_mdt, drx_mdt, CodeM, origen_police, qs-dispatch, lb-tablet or qb-mdt
- A quick action wheel that you can use while walking
- A wheel editor in the builder: order, menus, names, icons, department and rank limits, and your own actions
- Officers can pin favourite actions to the front of their wheel
- Bullet casings, blood and fingerprints at a scene, found with a flashlight and bagged as evidence
- Prints & DNA on the wheel, with matches against the evidence bags you carry. Gloves block the scan, and the codes copy or open the MDT profile
- Officers see each other on the map with their callsigns
- Callsigns set with /callsign or in the boss office
- Tackle a running suspect with G
- Live body cams and dash cams, watched from a station camera room or by supervisors from the wheel and the MDT
- Security cameras at banks and stores, using the camera numbers robbery scripts send
- Helicopter camera with night and thermal vision, lock-on and a spotlight
- Radar and plate reader in police cars, flagged plates and optional speed cameras
- Breathalyzer and drug swab
- Licences given and taken on the wheel
- Ankle monitors shown on the officer map
- qb-policejob's chat commands
- Search a car's trunk and glovebox
- Slow or stop traffic at a scene
- Officer-down alerts
- Police dogs that sniff, track, guard, bark and go after the person you look at
- Prints from people without gloves, on casings and cars, and dusting cars for prints
- A forensic lab at stations that names who evidence belongs to
- Bleach to clean up a scene
- Community service areas
- Booking mugshots, shown on the ID card
- Prison siren, prison breaks and solitary cells
- Duty clock in the boss office
- Shares flagged plates, BOLOs, mugshots, custody and warrants with XS-MDT
- Cuffs, escort, search, seize cash, ID check, GSR test, cars, plates, impound, cones, barriers, spike strips, fines, jail and a panic button
- Jail time that counts only while the prisoner is online, with prison jobs, a canteen and escape alerts
- Cuffs and jail time stay on through a logout or a crash
- Replaces qbx_police, qb-policejob and qb-prison
